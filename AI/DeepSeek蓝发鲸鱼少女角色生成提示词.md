# DeepSeek 蓝发鲸鱼少女角色生成提示词

## 用途

用于生成以 DeepSeek 为视觉主角的蓝发鲸鱼少女角色，适合技术知识卡片、漫画式信息图、产品介绍、Benchmark 对比和教育内容插图。

## 角色定位

DeepSeek 蓝发鲸鱼少女是一名可爱、聪明、活泼又略带调皮感的二次元 AI 吉祥物角色。她应当成为画面的主要视觉焦点，通过解释数据、展示结果、思考问题或庆祝成果与观众互动。

## 核心角色提示词

```text
Create a lovable chibi DeepSeek-inspired blue-haired whale girl character.

Character design:
- Long flowing blue hair with soft gradients from cobalt blue to sky blue
- A small whale-shaped hair accessory or whale-inspired side ornament
- Large expressive anime eyes with blue highlights
- Soft blush and a bright, friendly smile
- Cute, clever, confident and slightly mischievous personality
- White and blue hoodie or sailor-style outfit
- Subtle whale-inspired details in the clothing and accessories
- Small whale tail, fin or wave motifs used as decorative elements
- Clean, polished anime illustration with a handmade comic feeling
- Thick dark-blue or black outlines
- Soft highlights and gentle cel-shading
- Rounded chibi proportions and expressive body language

Personality:
- Clever technology expert
- Friendly educational guide
- Proud of her benchmark results
- Occasionally confused, playful or smug in a humorous way
- Approachable rather than intimidating

Possible poses:
- Explaining a chart with one hand raised
- Pointing proudly at a highlighted score
- Thinking with a question mark above her head
- Celebrating with stars, sparkles and a raised fist
- Smiling confidently with crossed arms
- Holding a small whale mascot or data card
- Reacting with surprise, embarrassment or cheerful mischief
```

## 漫画知识卡片适配版

```text
Use the DeepSeek blue-haired whale girl as the main character of a kawaii anime educational comic infographic.

She wears a white-and-blue hoodie or sailor-style outfit, has long blue hair,
large expressive blue eyes, a small whale hair accessory and subtle wave motifs.
Make her intelligent, cute, confident and slightly mischievous.

Show her explaining technical information through speech bubbles,
pointing at benchmark data, thinking about a difficult question,
celebrating an impressive result and proudly presenting the final conclusion.

Use a blue-and-white manga visual language,
thick dark outlines, soft cel-shading, bright highlights,
dynamic comic poses, expressive facial reactions,
question marks, exclamation marks, sparkles and energetic motion lines.

The DeepSeek character must be the dominant visual focus.
Supporting AI characters may react with surprise, confusion, admiration or excitement,
but they must remain secondary to DeepSeek.
```

## 角色外观约束

- 蓝色头发是最重要的识别特征，建议使用深钴蓝、天蓝和浅蓝渐变。
- 保留鲸鱼相关元素，但以发饰、服装纹样、尾鳍或波浪装饰为主，避免变成完整动物拟人替代少女角色。
- 服装以白色和蓝色为主，可加入少量浅紫、橙红或黄色作为强调色。
- 表情应具有明显情绪，避免普通站立、僵硬姿势和无表情状态。
- 角色整体应可爱、聪明、友好，不要设计成恐怖、冷峻或过度机械化的机器人。
- 如果用于知识卡片，应确保角色不会遮挡标题、表格、图表和关键数据。
- 可以使用 DeepSeek 启发的鲸鱼意象，但不要直接复制具体品牌吉祥物、商标或现有角色立绘。

## 配色建议

```text
Primary colors: cobalt blue, sky blue, white
Secondary colors: lavender, pale cyan, soft peach
Accent colors: warm red, orange, yellow
Outline colors: dark navy or black
```

## 负面提示词

```text
photorealistic human, realistic body proportions,
dark cyberpunk, horror, aggressive expression,
emotionless face, stiff standing pose, generic business character,
overly mechanical robot, full animal instead of a girl,
messy hair, muddy colors, excessive detail,
random brand logos, copied mascot design, watermark,
extra limbs, extra fingers, distorted hands,
character covering important text or data
```

## 使用建议

- 生成单独角色时，使用“核心角色提示词”，并指定动作、表情和画面比例。
- 生成知识卡片时，使用“漫画知识卡片适配版”，再补充具体主题和数据。
- 需要突出 DeepSeek 时，让她占据最大画面区域，并使用蓝色高光、星星、皇冠或结果卡片加强视觉权重。
- 需要制作连续漫画时，固定发色、鲸鱼发饰、服装配色和眼睛特征，只改变动作、表情与场景。
