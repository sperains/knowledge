# 日式可爱手绘知识卡片视觉语言提示词

## 用途

提取并复用一套适用于架构图、技术总结、教程、产品介绍、技术路线图和周报总结等主题的视觉语言。提示词只约束设计体系，不绑定具体主题、角色或版式。

## 核心风格提示词

```text
A kawaii Japanese hand-drawn infographic style.

Visual style:
- Japanese stationery and study notebook aesthetic
- Cute educational poster design
- Clean white paper background
- Soft pastel color palette
- Hand-drawn black ink outlines
- Rounded imperfect shapes
- Marker pen and colored pencil texture
- Playful doodle decorations
- Small stars, sparkles, arrows, stickers and handwritten annotations
- Friendly and approachable visual language

Layout style:
- Modern information visualization
- Clear card-based composition
- Structured grid layout
- Balanced whitespace
- Large handwritten-style title
- Rounded panels and soft borders
- Highlight important information with pastel color blocks
- Mix of typography and cute illustrations

Illustration style:
- Chibi kawaii characters
- Simple cute mascots
- Big expressive eyes
- Small decorative characters around the content
- Flat vector-like illustration mixed with hand-drawn feeling

Typography:
- Bold handwritten Japanese/Korean-style title lettering
- Casual handwritten annotations
- Thick rounded fonts
- High readability
- Friendly learning material feeling

Overall mood:
Cute, intelligent, playful, educational, modern,
like a viral tech knowledge card on Japanese social media.
```

## 风格增强词

### 手绘感

当生成结果过于接近商业扁平界面时，加入以下词语：

```text
rough hand-drawn lines,
slightly imperfect strokes,
sketchy ink outlines,
handmade illustration feeling
```

### 社交媒体知识卡片感

需要强化创作者知识卡片气质时，加入：

```text
Xiaohongshu style knowledge card,
social media infographic,
creator-made educational poster
```

### 科技内容适配

建议使用 `technology knowledge visualization`，避免直接写 `AI model comparison`，以免模型将画面锁定为人工智能模型对比表格。

## 使用约束

- 将主题内容与视觉风格分开描述；本笔记中的提示词只约束视觉语言。
- 适用于软件架构图、编程教程、产品介绍、技术路线图和周报总结等不同主题。
- “Do not copy any specific subject, characters, or layout. Only preserve the visual style.” 是关键约束，用于避免复刻参考图的具体内容和版式。
- 生成技术内容时，优先使用“technology knowledge visualization”等泛化描述，不要让具体主题词主导画面构成。
- 如果需要使用参考图，应明确要求提取视觉风格，而不是复制主体、角色、文字或布局。

## 风格定位

整体效果应接近“高级日式学习笔记”与“可爱科技知识信息图”的结合：清爽、易读、有手作温度，同时保留现代信息可视化的结构感。
