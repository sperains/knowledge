# 贴纸感新粗野主义网页设计提示词

## 用途

提取一套适用于产品页、数据页、作品集、博客、活动页、工具页、档案馆和社区页面的网页视觉语言。主题内容可以任意变化，但应保持统一的贴纸感新粗野主义界面风格。

## 风格定位

整体风格是“贴纸感新粗野主义”（Sticker-Sheet Neo-Brutalism）：暖奶油纸张、粗黑描边、硬偏移阴影、圆润展示字体、轻微旋转的彩色贴纸卡片，以及一本正经的幽默文案。

视觉公式：

> 奶油纸背景 + 粗黑描边 + 硬偏移阴影 + 圆润大字 + 橙黄粉蓝贴纸卡片 + 轻微倾斜

## 核心提示词

```text
Design a responsive web page about {subject} in a sticker-sheet neo-brutalist style.

Visual thesis:
- warm cream paper background
- thick dark ink outlines
- hard offset shadows with no blur
- rounded display typography
- pastel sticker-like cards with slight rotations
- one loud orange accent
- deadpan humor and playful editorial copy
- a small independent-project feeling rather than a corporate product feeling

Color palette:
- paper background: #FFF4DD
- ink: #26201A
- secondary ink: #5C5347
- muted ink: #877B6B
- loud orange accent: #FF5C2B
- sunny yellow: #FFD84D
- warm yellow card: #FFF0BD
- soft pink: #FFB9CC
- sky blue: #A5DCFF
- white card: #FFFDF7
- empty chart cell: #F1E3C4

Typography:
- use a rounded display font such as Baloo 2 Bold or ExtraBold for titles,
  labels, large status numbers and playful card headings
- use a clean system sans-serif for paragraphs
- use a monospace font only for timestamps, metadata, dates and small technical labels
- use large, friendly, compact headings rather than austere editorial typography

Shape and surface language:
- use 2px dark borders
- use hard shadows such as 4px 4px 0 #26201A or 6px 6px 0 #26201A
- use rounded corners around 14px, with pill-shaped buttons for actions
- rotate selected cards by approximately -1deg to +1deg
- use subtle paper grain or tiny dot texture in the background
- avoid blur, glassmorphism and soft floating shadows

Components:
- compact masthead with a circular avatar or sticker emblem
- friendly title and slightly italic tagline
- short explanatory paragraph near the top
- pill-shaped action buttons with thick outlines and hard shadows
- a hero status card with dotted texture and a yellow highlighted figure
- three colorful statistic tiles in yellow, pink and blue
- a compact chart, heatmap, calendar or timeline inside a bordered card
- archive entries as quote cards or preserved records
- small source links with orange underlines

Interaction:
- buttons physically depress on click by translating 3px and collapsing the shadow
- cards can slightly lift, rotate or bounce on hover
- icons can wiggle gently
- active states use orange, yellow or sky blue rather than gradients
- interactions should feel tactile, like handling printed stickers

Writing tone:
- serious information presented with dry, deadpan humor
- short, memorable labels
- combine plain explanation with playful phrases
- the page should feel lovingly over-focused on one small subject

Preserve the visual language only. Do not copy any specific brand, logo, text,
subject matter, data, illustration, layout or exact page structure from a reference.
```

## 网页生成模板

```text
Create a polished responsive web page for {subject}.

The page should answer: {primary_question}.
The main status is: {main_status}.
The key data includes: {data_types}.
The archive content includes: {archive_content}.
The main action is: {primary_action}.

Use a sticker-sheet neo-brutalist visual system:
warm cream paper background, thick dark outlines, hard offset shadows,
rounded Baloo 2-style display typography, clean sans-serif body text,
monospace metadata, loud orange accents, sunny yellow cards,
soft pink cards, sky blue cards, white dotted hero cards,
slight card rotations and tactile pill-shaped buttons.

Structure the page as:
1. compact masthead with a circular sticker/avatar
2. friendly title and italic tagline
3. concise explanation of the page's purpose
4. pill-shaped action buttons
5. prominent hero status card with a highlighted figure
6. three colorful statistic tiles
7. chart, heatmap, calendar or timeline card
8. archive, quote or history cards
9. minimal footer with source and update details

Use 2px dark borders, 14px rounded corners, hard black shadows,
slight rotations and low-complexity tactile interactions.

Avoid corporate polish. The result should feel like a charming independent
web project made from printed stickers and carefully arranged information.
```

## 内容变量

```text
{subject}          页面主题，例如“城市夜间电台”
{primary_question} 用户最关心的问题
{main_status}      当前最重要的状态或数字
{data_types}       统计、趋势、时间、分类或记录数据
{archive_content}   历史条目、引用、事件或用户内容
{primary_action}   主要行动，例如“订阅”“查看详情”“开始使用”
```

## 反向提示词

```text
Avoid pure white backgrounds, dark cyberpunk interfaces, neon gradients,
glassmorphism, blurred shadows, luxury editorial styling, austere minimalism,
sharp corporate dashboards, generic Bootstrap cards, oversized navigation,
all-black terminals, purple-blue AI gradients, 3D icons, photorealistic stock
illustrations, excessive rounded floating panels, and perfectly aligned sterile UI.
Do not make the page look like a conventional SaaS admin dashboard.
```

## 使用约束

- 不要把这套风格误写成纯终端风、赛博朋克或极简编辑风；核心是彩色贴纸卡片与粗黑硬阴影。
- 奶油色背景应占据主要面积，橙色、黄色、粉色和天蓝色作为局部贴纸色使用。
- 阴影必须是清晰的硬偏移阴影，不使用模糊阴影。
- 卡片可以轻微旋转，但整体仍需保持可读和可操作。
- 大标题和状态数字应圆润、友好、有视觉重量；正文保持简单易读。
- 主题内容可以完全更换，但应保留“解释区—状态卡—彩色数据卡—图表—档案卡”的叙事节奏。
- 如果使用参考图，只提取配色、字体、描边、阴影、圆角、旋转和交互质感，不复制具体内容或布局。

## 对话框与弹层风格

源站并不是传统的居中遮罩式模态框，而是采用“就地展开”的轻量对话框语言：操作发生在哪里，内容就在哪里展开。主要包括邮件订阅内联表单、图表单元格提示气泡和头像旁的消息气泡。

### 对话框提示词

```text
Use an inline sticker-style dialog rather than a conventional centered modal.

Dialog visual language:
- warm cream or white card background
- 2px dark ink outline
- 14px to 16px rounded corners
- hard offset shadow, approximately 3px to 4px in dark ink
- no blur, glassmorphism or translucent overlay
- compact padding and friendly rounded typography
- use yellow, pink or sky blue for the active state
- slightly playful rotation, at most about 1 degree
- keep the dialog attached to the triggering control or content

Interaction:
- expand in place instead of covering the page
- use a short scale-and-fade or slide-down transition
- buttons physically depress by translating 2px to 3px and collapsing the shadow
- preserve the surrounding page context
- close by toggling the same trigger or completing the action
```

### 三种可复用的对话框形态

#### 内联操作框

- 放在触发按钮下方，宽度随内容或容器展开。
- 输入框和提交按钮都采用粗描边、圆角和硬阴影。
- 不遮挡页面主体，不使用黑色全屏遮罩。
- 适合订阅、搜索、筛选、确认和短表单。

#### 图表提示气泡

- 深墨色背景，奶油色文字，等宽字体。
- 小尺寸、紧凑内边距，最大宽度约 260 像素。
- 位于目标元素正上方，用小三角指向目标。
- 只展示日期、状态、数量和一小段摘要。

#### 内容消息气泡

- 左侧连接圆形头像，右侧为白色或浅色气泡卡片。
- 气泡使用粗描边、硬阴影和圆角。
- 通过 CSS 三角形制作指向头像的尾巴。
- 时间使用黄色胶囊标签，正文使用普通无衬线字体，来源链接使用等宽字体。

### 对话框反向提示词

```text
Avoid centered enterprise modals, full-screen dark overlays, blurred backdrops,
glassmorphism, floating translucent panels, soft diffuse shadows, sharp square
alert boxes, dense form layouts, and generic browser-dialog styling.
```

## 风格检查清单

- 是否使用了暖奶油纸张背景？
- 是否存在粗黑描边和清晰硬阴影？
- 是否使用了黄色、橙色、粉色或天蓝色贴纸卡片？
- 标题是否圆润、友好，而不是冷峻的科技字体？
- 按钮是否有厚边框、圆角和按压反馈？
- 卡片是否有轻微倾斜或手工编排感？
- 信息是否像一个被认真制作的小型独立项目？
- 换成另一个主题后，是否仍然保留同样的贴纸感？
